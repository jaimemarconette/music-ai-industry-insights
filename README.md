# Music & AI Industry Insights

Exploring the intersection of **artificial intelligence, music consumption, creators, rights, and the music business**.

## About This Project

This project examines how advances in AI are changing the music ecosystem and the implications for artists, creators, consumers, labels, publishers, and technology companies.

The goal is not to predict a single future for AI and music, but to use publicly available data and industry research to identify meaningful signals, emerging opportunities, and strategic questions.

## Key Questions

* How is AI changing the way music is created, discovered, distributed, and consumed?
* What opportunities and challenges does generative AI create for artists and creators?
* How might AI affect the economics of recorded music and music publishing?
* What considerations should technology companies account for when building music products?
* How can consumption and audience-impact data inform strategic decisions around music and licensing?

## Approach

The project combines:

* Publicly available music-industry and consumption data reports authored by Jaime Marconette
* Data analysis and visualization
* Market and consumer trends
* Strategic analysis of implications for the music ecosystem

All data and sources used in the analysis will be documented within the project.

## About the Author

**Jaime Marconette** is a music and technology executive with experience spanning music industry strategy, data and analytics, digital marketing, artist and consumer engagement, strategic consulting, and industry relations.

He currently leads Music Insights & Industry Relations at Luminate and previously served as Director of Digital Strategy at Sony Music Nashville.

His work sits at the intersection of **music, technology, data, and the evolving creator economy**.
